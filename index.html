Mechanism · How it works

Three steps to deliver trust to you.

We don’t move information — we build trust. From source factories to last-mile installation, every link in the chain is watched over. At Provenir, every connection is worth entrusting.

⟁  01 / 03

Présenter

On-site curation

Showrooms across 9 cities, personally visited by the Provenir team — production lines, materials, craftsmanship, and compliance are all inspected. Only verified source manufacturers make it into the showroom.

⟁  02 / 03

Connecter

Precise matching

Global designers connect directly with factory principals in our vertical community — intelligently matched by category, capacity, and craft intent. Less detours, the right people find each other in the first round.

⟁  03 / 03

Accompagner

Full escort

From sampling to mass production, cross-border logistics, and last-mile installation — our professional team follows through every step. Every link is covered, delivering the sincerity of Chinese manufacturing intact.
