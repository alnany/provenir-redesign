A luxurious sofa that blends the past and present

In the soft luxury of modern leather, subtle Eastern influences are woven in, with light grey cushions serving as the finishing touch, connecting the traditional and contemporary aesthetics of the space.

Fluid lines trace a relaxed silhouette; rounded armrests and plush cushions lend an air of ease. Set against a screen painted with grasses and trees, a quiet Eastern sensibility folds into the soft luxury of modern leather — light-grey cushions act as the finishing touch, threading a dialogue between the traditional and the contemporary.
